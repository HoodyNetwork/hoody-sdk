 ▾ How do I open an alias at my own domain?
   Where you manage your DNS, add a CNAME record:

   - Name: the part you want in front of your domain, such as
   app.
   - Target: the address in the alias's "Your domain points to"
   column.
   - DNS proxy: off for that record.

   HTTPS is set up on the first visit, and the computer's access
   rules still apply.                                                         █
